# Appurtenance Categories & Special Considerations

**Both lists below are starting points, not exhaustive.** Apply additional industry-standard considerations for the specific property, peril, and project conditions.

## Appurtenance categories to inventory

- **Siding / wall cladding** (vinyl, fiber-cement, wood, brick veneer, stucco)
- **Fencing** (wood, vinyl, chain-link, wrought iron, composite)
- **Decks, porches, patios, screened enclosures**
- **Pools, hot tubs, pool enclosures, pool decks**
- **Detached structures** (sheds, detached garage, workshop, gazebo, pergola)
- **Landscaping** (trees, shrubs, sod, mulch, retaining walls)
- **Hardscape** (driveway, walkway, patio paving)
- **Site features** (outdoor kitchen, fire pit, outdoor lighting, irrigation, gates, mailbox, signage)

## Special considerations by appurtenance

- **Siding** — surface damage often hides damaged housewrap, sheathing, and even framing. Photos from inside (during interior work) can confirm. Matching language across continuous elevations is critical.
- **Fencing** — measure exact lineal footage; concrete-set posts are a separate labor line; gate hardware is often missed; automated entry systems are a high-dollar omission.
- **Decks** — structural posts/beams/joists vs. surface boards. Composite vs. wood. Railings, balusters, post caps. Stairs, stringers. Flashing where deck meets house.
- **Pools** — liner replacement, coping, deck around pool, pool equipment (pump, filter, heater, cleaner), enclosure screens.
- **Landscaping** — trees usually have a per-tree value cap in the policy; use `Read` on the policy to verify. Sod, irrigation lines, low-voltage lighting often overlooked.
