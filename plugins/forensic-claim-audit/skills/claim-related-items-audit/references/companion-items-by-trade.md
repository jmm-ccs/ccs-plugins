# Companion Items by Trade

A line item rarely stands alone in real construction. Installing the primary item requires companion items that the carrier's macro often misses.

**This list is a starting point, not exhaustive.** Apply any other reliable, independently-verifiable, industry-standard guidance for the trade and the specific project.

## Flooring (any kind)

Subfloor inspection/repair, floor leveling compound, adhesive, underlayment, transition strips at doorways, T-mold or reducers, vapor barrier (where applicable), and the matching trim (shoe mold, quarter round).

## Tile

Thinset, grout, sealer, backer board, expansion strip, schluter trim where elevation changes.

## Drywall

Tape, mud (multiple coats), corner bead, primer, texture matching.

## Paint

Primer, caulk, masking, surface prep (wash/sand).

## Cabinetry

Shims, fillers, scribe molding, hardware (knobs/pulls), under-cabinet trim.

## Trim / baseboard

Caulk at top edge and corners, paintable filler at nail holes, primer/paint coats.

## Plumbing fixtures

Supply lines, shut-off valves, escutcheons, p-traps, wax rings on toilets.

## Electrical fixtures

Junction boxes, wire nuts, dimmer compatibility, neutral wire requirement on smart switches.

## Roofing

Starter strip, ridge cap, drip edge, ice & water shield where required by code, pipe boots, valley flashing, ridge vent, hip and ridge shingles.

## Siding

Housewrap, J-channel, flashing, corner posts, fasteners, sealant.

## HVAC R&R

Refrigerant recovery/recharge, line set re-insulation, condensate drain.
