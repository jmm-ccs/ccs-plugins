# Frequently-Missed Line Item Categories

Adapted from Forensic Claim Analysis Checklist 3 — categories of micro-operations carrier macros routinely omit.

**This list is a starting point, not exhaustive.** Apply any other reliable, independently-verifiable, industry-standard guidance for the specific project.

## Site protection

Protective blocking under furniture, floor protection paper, dust control barriers, heavy-duty masking. Must be broken down by trade order of operations, not bundled into a single line. Different trades require different protection (e.g., masking for painting is distinct from floor protection for drywall installation).

## Hardware & fixtures

LED bulbs, blind R&R, occupancy sensors, cabinet accessories, toilet seats, drawer pulls, knob hardware. Small unit cost individually; large aggregate when multiplied across the home. Modern smart-home fixtures (motion sensors, smart switches with neutrals) cost materially more than legacy fixtures and are often scoped at legacy prices.

## Preparatory labor

Operations that real installers perform but carrier macros assume away:

- Planing/retrofitting doors after flooring height changes
- Cleaning walls before paint
- Detach-and-reset appliances
- Caulk removal before re-caulking
- Surface prep (scuff, sand) before primer
- Removing old hardware/fixtures before new install

Labor is never free. Each prep operation is a billable line.

## Waste & debris

- Dumpster rental (size, count, swap-outs)
- Heavy debris removal — drywall, flooring, tile, concrete (each a different code, each priced differently per ton)
- Specialized hauling (mattresses, appliances with refrigerant, tires, electronics)
- Tipping fees and tonnage overage charges
- Loading labor (separate from dumpster rental)

Convert removed-material square footage to tonnage to support the right debris code; do not just price the dumpster rental.
