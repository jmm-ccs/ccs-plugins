# During-Construction Protections & Post-Construction Cleanup

**Both lists below are starting points, not exhaustive.** Apply additional industry-standard protections and cleanup operations appropriate to the trade, project conditions, and occupant situation.

## During-construction protections

- **Dust barriers / poly containment** at room thresholds, especially when sanding floors, drywall, or plaster.
- **Negative-air machines** (HEPA-filtered) for high-dust operations and to keep airborne particulates out of unaffected zones. Same equipment family as IICRC-aligned air scrubbers in water remediation.
- **HVAC return masking** during sanding/dust operations to prevent contamination of the duct system.
- **Floor protection** in transit paths (paper, hardboard, ram board) — already partially flagged in the Continuity Audit; reconcile here.
- **Wall corner protection** at transit-path corners.
- **Window covering** (poly + tape) when adjacent exterior work generates debris.
- **Doorway zip-walls / dust doors** for surgical containment.
- **Cabinet and fixture covering** in rooms where overhead work continues with cabinets in place.
- **Occupant-in-place considerations** — if the occupants will be living in the home during construction, additional sanitation, fume-control coatings (low-VOC), and after-hours work scheduling may be appropriate.

## Post-construction cleanup

After the trades finish, the home requires a level of cleaning above ordinary housekeeping:

- **Final construction clean** — detail of every surface, removal of tape residue, point-up paint touchup. Distinct from a routine maid service — different code.
- **Duct cleaning** (NADCA-aligned scope) — required after most fire claims, often appropriate after major renovations or any IAQ event.
- **HVAC filter replacement**.
- **HEPA vacuuming** of soft surfaces (carpet, upholstery left in place).
- **Air quality testing** post-restoration — required in S520 mold work (PRV) and prudent after fire/smoke claims. Use `WebSearch` to verify current S520 requirements.
- **Window cleaning** inside and out where construction debris settled.
- **Light-fixture cleaning** when overhead dust accumulated.
- **Cabinet interior cleaning**.
