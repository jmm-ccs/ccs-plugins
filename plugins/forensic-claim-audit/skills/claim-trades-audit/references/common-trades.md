# Common Residential Trades

**This list is a starting point, not exhaustive.** Pick what applies to the specific project, do not include trades you cannot tie to a real line item, and add additional trades the project requires.

- General Carpentry / Rough Carpentry
- Finish Carpentry / Trim
- Drywall
- Painting
- Flooring (often subdivided: Carpet, Hardwood, Tile, Vinyl)
- Tile (sometimes its own trade)
- Cabinetry
- Countertops
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- HVAC / Mechanical
- Roofing
- Siding / Exterior Cladding
- Gutter / Downspout
- Insulation
- Masonry
- Concrete / Foundation
- Glazing / Windows
- Doors
- Hardware / Locksmith
- Cleaning (final clean, duct cleaning)
- Restoration (water mitigation, fire/smoke cleanup)
- Demolition / Debris Removal
- Pest / Mold Remediation
- Landscaping / Hardscape
- Fencing
- Pool / Spa
- Solar
- Specialty (asbestos, lead RRP, etc.)
